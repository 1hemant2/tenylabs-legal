Privacy Policy

Duo Desk Puzzle — Teny Labs

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Teny Labs ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile game, Duo Desk Puzzle.

1. Information We Collect

Duo Desk Puzzle does not require account registration or the submission of personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

The game may store gameplay progress, preferences, and settings locally on your device. We do not operate a game account or cloud-sync service to receive this locally stored information.

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we receive the contact information and message you provide.

2. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of Duo Desk Puzzle does not display advertisements or intentionally integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

We may introduce advertisements or analytics in future versions. If we do, we will update this Privacy Policy and applicable app-store disclosures before releasing those changes.

3. How Information Is Used

Locally stored gameplay information is used to save your progress and game preferences.

Information you voluntarily provide through support enquiries is used to respond to your questions and resolve reported problems.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information or share locally stored gameplay data with advertisers.

Support emails are processed by the email services involved in delivering your messages. Your operating system and app store may also process information under their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Duo Desk Puzzle is intended for a broad audience, including players aged 9 and above.

The game does not require children to create accounts or provide personal information to play. The current version does not display advertisements.

Parents or guardians may contact us if they believe a child has shared personal information through a support enquiry and wish to request its deletion.

6. Data Storage and Deletion

Game progress and preferences are generally stored locally on your device. You can remove this information by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for handling enquiries, maintaining appropriate records, or meeting applicable legal obligations.

7. Data Security

We aim to minimise the information handled by Duo Desk Puzzle and use the device's application storage mechanisms for locally saved information. However, no electronic storage or communication system is completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game's features, data-handling practices, or applicable requirements change.

Any changes will be published on this page, along with an updated revision date.
